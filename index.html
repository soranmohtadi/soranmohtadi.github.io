I study how energy markets, natural resources, and technological change shape economic development, inequality, and public policy.

I'm a postdoctoral fellow at the Hobby School of Public Affairs, University of Houston. My research spans applied microeconomics, energy and environmental economics, political economy, and development economics.

Current projects examine AI and electricity demand, energy transitions, and the distributional consequences of energy policies.
