I study who wins and who loses when energy markets, institutions, and policies change.

I'm a postdoctoral fellow at the Hobby School of Public Affairs, University of Houston. Using applied econometric methods, I work on the energy transition, resource governance, and sustainable development.

What I work on

  1. Energy and inequality

    How energy price shocks and natural resource dependence shape income distribution, education, and labor markets.

  2. AI, digitalization and electricity

    The implications of digitalization and data centers for electricity demand, emissions, and environmental policy.

  3. Energy transitions

    How policy, firms, and institutions respond to decarbonization, including gender and labor-market outcomes.

  4. Institutions and development

    Natural resources, political institutions, human capital, and economic development.
